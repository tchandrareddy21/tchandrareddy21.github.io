## Introduction

A machine learning system to classify and predict customer complaints for financial products, enabling quick identification of problematic complaints and helping companies take proactive action.

## Approach

Use existing financial product complaints to train a model that identifies potentially problematic new complaints, helping the company take action to resolve issues.

## Pipeline

- ML-based complaint classification
- Automated pipeline with Apache Airflow
- Docker packaging and CircleCI integration

## Results

The four-month project delivered automated complaint triage. The original portfolio did not include numerical evaluation results.

## Conclusion

Automating complaint classification supports more consistent triage and helps teams prioritize customer issues.
